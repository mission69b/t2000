# Creative brief — founder / brand social (X)

**For:** marketing  
**From:** product  
**Attach:** [`MARKETING-ONEPAGER.md`](./MARKETING-ONEPAGER.md) (product facts, two brands, word list)  
**Goal:** ice the timeline after a quiet period; own **agent economy on Sui** mindshare; stay **soft on t2000 product launch** unless product says go  
**Channels:** primarily **X / Twitter** (founder voice + brand, same rules)

---

## 1. Job of the content

| Priority | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sound like a **builder with a thesis**, not a launch PR robot |
| 2 | Make **agent economy + settlement / receipts / USDC / Sui** feel inevitable |
| 3 | Leave room for curiosity (links optional; full product announcement is **out of scope** until instructed) |
| 4 | When product appears, use one-pager language — hire · work · earn, not feature spam |

**Not the job:** grow vanity through engagement bait, “who’s building this?”, AI-thread numbered lists, or soft product laundry.

---

## 2. Voice

| Yes | No |
|---|---|
| Short paragraphs (2–4 lines), essay fragments | Long threads 1/14 of hot takes |
| Systems / inevitability (“is / isn’t”) | Hype adjectives (revolutionize, game-changing) |
| Concrete money primitives (escrow, receipt, settle, $0 claim) | Vague “empowering agents” |
| Dry / deadpan where it fits | Sorry for not posting / “back from the void” |
| One idea per post | Packing Sui + t2000 + Connect + fees + tagline in one tweet |

**Cadence:** 3–5 posts/week is enough. Quality over schedule theatrics.

**Brand split on social:**
- **t2000 / economy rails:** agent work, USDC, escrow, receipts, Sui  
- **Audric:** AI you can put to work — marketplace from chat; private chat / inference second (one-pager lines)  
- Don’t collapse brands; don’t invent money rails (marketplace settles **USDC**, not Stripe)

---

Use these as **tone anchors**, not templates to spam.

**C — one-pager product lines (use when soft-product or reply):**  
See MARKETING-ONEPAGER — t2000 title/support/tagline; Audric three locked lines.  
Do **not** lead long periods with full marketplace “announcement” copy until founder green-lights.

---

## 4. Themes to invent into (original writing required)

Marketing owns original posts under these **themes**. Do not rewrite third-party viral Sui threads.

1. **Agent economy = settlement, not chat**  
2. **Verifiability / receipts** (facts counterparties can’t edit)  
3. **Earn-first / $0 entry** (open work with budget already escrowed — accurate)  
4. **Speed / constraints agents care about** (without copying someone else’s “agents will choose the fastest rail” essay)  
5. **Sui as home for this economy** (tag @SuiNetwork only when it earns it; not every post)  
6. **Models vs chains** (intelligence cheap; coordination of money is not)  

Each post: **one theme**.

---

## 5. Hard product accuracy (must not mess up)

From product / one-pager — if unsure, ask:

- **Earn-first is real:** claim Open jobs can start with **$0** USDC; buyer already escrowed.  
- Delivery of jobs is **text / markdown** (links OK); not “attach PNG as job body.”  
- **Fees:** services 5% on settle from seller payout; x402 no protocol fee — only if fees are mentioned.  
- **Passport Connect** is how the market runs in Claude — product is the market, not “limits.”  
- **Don’t claim** “live in Claude’s official directory” unless confirmed.  
- **SSO / one login desk+Claude** not shipped as seamless — don’t promise it.  
- **t2000 Money story for economy posts = USDC.** Stripe/plan is not the agent economy pitch.

---

## 6. Anti-patterns (automatic reject)

- Leading with **“who’s building this?”** (founder cosplay; weak)  
- **Sorry I haven’t posted** ice-breakers  
- Numbered AI hype threads with emoji headers  
- “Game-changing / revolutionary / the future is now”  
- Collaging competitor wording or hot tweets as our own  
- Full **t2000 marketplace launch** thread without written OK  
- Calling t2000 a **store**  
- Audric = job escrow in Stripe

---

## 8. Evaluation bar (founder)

A draft is good if:
1. It could sit under a real builder’s name without cringe  
2. One sentence would still work standalone  
3. It teaches a true constraint of agent commerce  
4. It does not sound like Claude wrote a Twitter strategy  

A draft is bad if it needs “who’s building this?”, stacks buzzwords, or is interchangeable with generic crypto Twitter.
