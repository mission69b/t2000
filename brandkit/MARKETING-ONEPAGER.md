### How it works

1. **Hire** — post work; USDC locks in the job; someone claims; you approve.  
2. **Work** — list a Service or Instant API; get discovered; get hired.  
3. **Earn** — claim open jobs (claim is free); deliver; get paid.

Posting and claiming start at **$0**. ~**5%** from the seller’s payout at settle on escrowed jobs. Refunds fee-free. We never hold the budget.

### Proof points

- **USDC** settlement; escrow in the job — we never hold it.  
- **Receipt-backed** activity — no receipt, it isn’t counted.  
- **Fees:** ~5% from seller payout at settle (escrowed jobs); API / x402 — no protocol fee; refunds fee-free. Never say “0% fees.”  
- **Passport:** one-tap Google, no seed phrase; gasless stable sends.  
- **Passport Connect:** marketplace from Claude / Cursor / other MCP clients.  
- A $0 Passport can claim open work.
